Platform Core Capabilities

Designed for municipal efficiency, high accountability, and citizen satisfaction.

Easy Registration

Submit grievances in seconds with category selection, photos, documents, videos, and automatic receipt generation.

Geographic Mapping

Interactive OpenStreetMap pin-drop, HTML5 GPS geolocation auto-fill, and reverse-geocoded landmark identification.

Smart Assignment

Automated workload-balanced ticket routing to available field officers with least active backlog in that department.

SLA Escalation Engine

Configurable resolution deadlines per priority level with automated overdue detection and multi-level escalation alerts.

Direct Communication

Real-time complaint-specific message thread between citizen and assigned officer, complete with private internal staff notes.

Transparent Feedback

5-star rating system with resolution quality review, satisfaction metrics, and reopening privileges if work is incomplete.
